# Mapping: ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A (controls)

Annex A's controls are policy and process artifacts; fovea's cells are
threat-space. The mapping is *visible coverage*, not "this control solves
this threat":

- **Organizational controls (~1–37)** — most map to `admin` and to
  `socio_legal` cells, and to the trust-anchor register's ceremony rules;
  e.g. "Policies for information security", "Segregation of duties" concern
  the `admin` column's key-ceremony cells.
- **People controls (~1–7)** — background checks, contracts, awareness …
  map to `trusted_partner` and `internal` actors, and to gaps in a fovea
  `org`-tagged measure that an ISMS would fill.
- **Physical controls (~1–14)** — facilities, equipment, utilities …
  map naturally to `physical_natural` cells (and to `decommission` cells
  covering disposal).
- **Technological controls (~1–50)** — the obvious bulk: cryptographic
  controls, network security, secure development, identity management,
  logging, etc. They show up as fovea countermeasure `by_design` claims or
  as `org` obligations, with the cell providing the *threat that motivates*
  them.

The mapping annex is for auditors: a mapped matrix lets them see "where is
control X satisfied" without claiming that fovea replaces an ISMS.
